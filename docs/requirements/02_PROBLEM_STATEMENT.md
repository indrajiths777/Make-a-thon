# 2. Problem Statement – Brightloop Software

## 2.1 Current State Challenges
Brightloop Software currently manages recurring revenue operations through fragmented, manual workflows. This legacy approach suffers from critical operational bottlenecks:

1. **Spreadsheet-Based Subscription Tracking**:
   - Customer subscriptions, renewals, plan durations, and seat counts are tracked in disconnected spreadsheets across multiple staff members.
   - Discrepancies and sync conflicts frequently occur, resulting in untracked plan modifications and unbilled services.

2. **Manual and Error-Prone Invoicing**:
   - Monthly and yearly invoices are manually drafted, calculated, and dispatched.
   - Manual calculation of product variant surcharges (e.g. brand-specific premiums such as Odoo brand adding +560) and compound tax rates introduces billing errors and customer disputes.
   - Crucially, billing days are missed, or worse, duplicate invoices are dispatched for the same period.

3. **Untracked Renewals and Revenue Churn**:
   - Contract expiration and renewal dates rely entirely on ad-hoc staff vigilance.
   - Subscriptions lapse without proactive outreach or automatic recurring invoice generation, directly causing revenue leakage.

4. **Inefficient Payment Reconciliation**:
   - Payments arriving via various channels (bank transfer, credit card, manual wire) are matched by manually scrolling bank statements and inspecting unstructured notes.
   - Partial payments are either lost or improperly tracked, leaving true accounts receivable obscured.

5. **Lack of Real-Time Financial Visibility**:
   - The finance leadership currently spends days at the end of each fiscal quarter reconciling records to calculate Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR), outstanding receivables, and overdue invoices.
   - Executive decision-making is severely delayed due to the lack of real-time dashboards and analytics.

6. **Poor Customer Experience**:
   - Customers have no direct self-service mechanism to view their active plans, download past tax invoices, track payment status, or review upcoming renewals without emailing support staff.

## 2.2 Urgency of Resolution
Without an integrated, automated software solution, Brightloop Software cannot scale its customer acquisition, enforce contractual payment terms, or guarantee financial audit compliance.
