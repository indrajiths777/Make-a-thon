# 1. Project Overview – Brightloop Software Subscription Management System

## 1.1 Executive Summary
Brightloop Software is a rapidly expanding B2B and B2C software company providing digital business solutions, software add-ons, and multi-tier enterprise platforms. As Brightloop’s customer base scales from small retail shops on flexible monthly subscriptions to multinational enterprises on annual multi-seat agreements, the manual, spreadsheet-driven administrative process has become unsustainable.

The **Subscription Management System (SMS)** is an enterprise-grade digital backbone engineered to orchestrate the entire subscription lifecycle. It unifies catalog management, automated recurring billing, quotation workflows, dynamic discounting, taxation, payment recording, and executive financial reporting into a cohesive, secure platform.

## 1.2 Enterprise Context
- **Organization**: Brightloop Software
- **Business Model**: Recurring SaaS (Software as a Service) subscriptions, modular software add-ons, and tiered product variant licensing.
- **Billing Frequencies**: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly recurring cadences.
- **Customer Segmentation**:
  - Small businesses requiring self-serve portal capabilities, transparent billing, and instant receipt generation.
  - Mid-market and enterprise clients requiring negotiated quotation templates, multi-line product configurations, customized payment terms, and staff-assisted account management.

## 1.3 Key Architectural Pillars
1. **Financial Integrity & Data Consistency**: Zero tolerance for skipped or duplicate recurring billing cycles, preserving immutable agreed pricing on active contracts and historical invoices.
2. **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Strict multi-tenant operational boundaries segregating Administrator governance, Staff daily operations, and Customer self-service portals.
3. **Automated Lifecycle Transitions**: Seamless progression across Draft, Quotation, Confirmed, Active, and Closed subscription states, coupled with automated recurring invoice generation.
4. **Auditability & Compliance**: Full historical ledgering of payments, partial payments, credit adjustments, and tax calculations.
